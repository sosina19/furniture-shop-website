# SEO

## Goals

Make Bzuayehu Furniture easier to discover when people search for the business or relevant furniture/products.

## Public page requirements

- Unique page titles
- Useful meta descriptions
- Correct heading hierarchy
- Stable and descriptive URLs
- Descriptive image alt text
- Mobile-friendly pages
- Fast pages
- Internal linking

## Product SEO

Product supports:

- `seo_title`
- `seo_description`

Use stable product URLs such as:

```text
/products/modern-sofa
```

## Images

Alt text should describe the image itself.

Good:

```text
Modern gray three-seat sofa
```

Avoid keyword stuffing.

## Search engine infrastructure

Plan for:

- `sitemap.xml`
- `robots.txt`
- Google Search Console
- Appropriate structured data
